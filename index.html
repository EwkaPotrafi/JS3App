Before you rely on this

This app reproduces the arms, limits and formulas in JS3 Weight and Balance Calculator v14 for the serial number in the profile. It is a convenience tool, not an approved document. The signed weighing report and the flight manual are authoritative. Re-check the numbers against the manufacturer's sheet after any weighing, any equipment change, and after any change to this profile.
